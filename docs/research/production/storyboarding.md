## 1. Purpose

Storyboarding externalizes visual storytelling before expensive final production, enabling pacing, framing, camera planning, and iterative feedback.

## 2. Professional Terminology

- Storyboard: rough visual planning representation used to pitch and refine story ideas. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/v/pitching-feedback
- Story reel: rough video draft that combines storyboards with dialogue, sound effects, and music. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch
- Pitching: sharing storyboarded story form to collect feedback and improve narrative. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch
- Feedback: critique input used to revise storyboards. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch
- Scenes and panels (software practice): board work is decomposed into scene/panel structures with timing workflows. Source: https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/getting-started/introduction.html

## 3. Professional Definitions

- Storyboarding is a planning stage, not final render output, and is used repeatedly with pitching and feedback loops. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/v/pitching-feedback
- Storyboards can become timing-aware story reels/animatic forms with audio context. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch and https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/getting-started/animatic.html
- Storyboard tools are designed around camera, timing, sound, and export operations. Source: https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/book/user-guide/about-user-guide.html

## 4. Typical Production Workflow

- Script/outline informs scene and beat decomposition. Source: https://www.finaldraft.com/learn/going-from-outline-to-script/
- Storyboards are drafted for key beats and shot progression.
- Boards are pitched for feedback and revised in multiple passes. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/v/pitching-feedback
- Timing is refined into story reel/animatic and audio alignment. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch and https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/release-notes/storyboard-pro-27-release-notes.html
- Editorial review validates sequencing and pacing. Source: https://help.autodesk.com/view/SGSUB/ENU/

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

- Story artists / storyboard artists: visualize narrative and shot logic.
- Director / creative lead: set intent, approve staging and pacing.
- Editorial: evaluate timing and continuity in cut context. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/v/pitching-feedback
- Production tracking roles: coordinate review and approvals at project level. Source: https://help.autodesk.com/view/SGSUB/ENU/

## 6. Inputs

- Script scenes and action lines. Source: https://www.finaldraft.com/learn/screenplay-formatting-elements/
- Story beats and sequence-level intent. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/film-grammar/a/glossary-grammar
- Character and environment references.
- Audio placeholders and dialogue drafts. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch

## 7. Outputs / Deliverables

- Boarded panels and shot order.
- Camera and staging intent.
- Story reel/animatic with preliminary timing and sound context. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch and https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/release-notes/storyboard-pro-27-release-notes.html
- Revision notes and approval status.

## 8. Production Records / Data

- Panel-level timing tables and review adjustments. Source: https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/release-notes/storyboard-pro-27-release-notes.html
- Script/board linkage and notes. Source: https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/book/user-guide/about-user-guide.html
- Review and approval workflow records. Source: https://help.autodesk.com/view/SGSUB/ENU/

## 9. Lifecycle / State Changes

- Initial board draft -> pitched board -> feedback revisions -> story reel/animatic -> editorial-informed revisions -> approved previsualization package.

## 10. Relationships to Other Production Concepts

- Story and Scene: storyboards convert written scene intent into visual sequencing.
- Shot: storyboards propose shot logic before final capture/render.
- Continuity: board revisions preserve narrative coherence and character placement.
- Edit: story reel and animatic bridge planning to editorial timing.
- Asset: character/environment references constrain board consistency.

## 11. Stable Industry Concepts

- Storyboarding is iterative.
- Feedback loops are integral to board quality.
- Storyboard quality is tied to narrative clarity and timing, not image polish alone.

## 12. Studio-Specific or Variable Practices

- Panel conventions and file structures vary by studio/tooling.
- Degree of board detail before animatic varies by production type.
- Some studios pitch from rough thumbnails; others from near-clean boards.

## 13. Research Findings

- Finding: Pixar/Khan material describes repeated storyboarding, pitching, feedback, and re-boarding before downstream editorial. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/v/pitching-feedback
- Finding: Story reel definition includes dialogue, SFX, and music overlays on storyboard content. Source: https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/storyboard-your-film/a/glossary-pitch
- Finding: Storyboard Pro documents explicit capabilities for timing, camera, sound, supervision, and export. Source: https://docs.toonboom.com/help/storyboard-pro-27/storyboard/book/user-guide/about-user-guide.html

## 14. Research Principles

- Principle: Storyboard is a first-class planning and review artifact, not just image generation output.
- Principle: Iterative feedback must be represented in storyboard lifecycle state.
- Principle: Storyboard artifacts should preserve linkage to scene and shot intent.
